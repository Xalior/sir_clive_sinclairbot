Good day! Here is how to talk to me.

**Start a conversation:** tag me – "@Sir. Clive Sinclairbot" – in the channel, with your question.

**Continue it:** reply to my answer. You do not need to tag me again. I remember the earlier messages of that conversation.

**Go back to an earlier point:** reply to one of my earlier answers in the conversation. I continue from that answer and forget what came after it, but the original conversation stays as it was. Reply to my latest answer in it to carry on there.

**Ask about someone else's message:** reply to that message and tag me. I read the message you replied to before your question.

If someone else replies to one of my answers, they start their own conversation with me. Conversations expire after 10 days. After that, tag me to start a new one.
